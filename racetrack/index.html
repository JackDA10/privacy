Racetrack: IFR Hold Trainer

This app collects no data about you and makes no network connections of its own.

What this app stores

Two small values, on your device only, and both exist for one purpose: to ask you once whether you would like to rate the app, and never ask again. One counts how many entry problems you have answered correctly; the other records that the request has already been made. That is the entire contents.

Your progress is not saved. Lesson position, quiz scores, the holds you have worked, which entries you got right — none of it is written down. It lives in memory while the app is open and starts fresh the next time you launch it. The app has no account, no identifier, and no file it writes.

Because the two values are so small and so narrow, there is no clearing control inside the app; there would be nothing meaningful to clear. Deleting the app removes them.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests — every lesson, diagram, hold and quiz question is inside the app, and it works fully in airplane mode
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or health access — the app has no permission prompt at all, and it does not read your GPS position
  • No purchases or subscriptions

Links out of the app

The only links in the app are the Support and Privacy Policy items in the Lessons menu, and they open this page. Nothing else leaves the app. The rating request is handled by iOS itself, in Apple's own dialog: the app asks the system to show it and receives no answer back, so it never learns whether you rated it or what you said.

Support

Questions, bugs, or an entry you think the app has wrong: admin@flywithautopilot.com. Say which screen and which hold — the fix, the inbound course, the turn direction and your heading — and include your device and iOS version.

Racetrack teaches holding pattern entries: reading a hold out of a clearance or off a chart, picking direct, parallel or teardrop, and flying the wind correction. Use it to learn the method, not as the authority. The entry procedures it teaches are the ones the AIM recommends rather than regulation, the sector boundaries it draws are a teaching aid rather than a tolerance, and the hold you are actually cleared into is the one ATC gave you or the one printed on the chart. Fly the clearance you have.
