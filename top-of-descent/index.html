Top of Descent Calculator

This app collects no data about you, makes no network connections of its own, and stores nothing at all.

What this app stores

Nothing. Not on a server, and not on your device either. This app is a calculator rather than a course, so it has no progress to keep and no score to remember. The altitudes and speeds you type live only as long as the app is open; close it and it starts again at its defaults. There is no account, no identifier, and no file it writes.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests — every figure it shows is arithmetic done on your device, and it works fully in airplane mode
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or health access — the app has no permission prompt at all, and it does not read your GPS position
  • No purchases or subscriptions

Links to other apps

The More from AutoPilot screen lists the other apps in this family and links to the App Store and to flywithautopilot.com. Tapping one hands that link to your browser or to the App Store app. The app itself sends nothing: no identifier, no numbers you entered, nothing about you travels with that link. Once the page is open, that site's privacy practices apply rather than this one's.

Support

Questions, bugs, or a number you think the app has wrong: jackdeeganpost@gmail.com. Say which screen, and include the altitudes and speeds you had entered along with your device and iOS version.

Top of Descent Calculator works out where a descent has to begin and how fast it has to come down — feet per nautical mile, feet per minute, the angle that makes, and the point on the profile where you start down. It is a planning aid. It is not a navigation source, and it is no substitute for the published procedure or for your clearance: the chart, ATC and your aircraft's own performance decide what you actually fly. Fly the airplane first.
