Privacy Policy

These apps collect no data about you. None. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind.

What is collected

Nothing. No personal information, no usage data, no device identifiers, no diagnostics, no location, no contacts. Not anonymously, not in aggregate, not at all.

What is stored, and where

The apps remember your place — which lessons you have opened and your best quiz scores. That is written to your device's own storage and nowhere else. It is never transmitted, because the apps contain no networking code capable of transmitting it.

Deleting an app deletes everything it stored. Every app also has a reset control in its settings if you want to clear your progress without deleting the app.

Third parties

There are none. The apps link no third-party SDKs, no analytics libraries, no advertising or attribution frameworks, and no crash reporters. Nobody else receives anything about you, because nothing is sent anywhere.

Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children. These apps are safe for users of any age in this respect.

Your rights

Data-protection law gives you rights to access, correct, export and delete the personal data a company holds about you. Here that is straightforward: no personal data about you is held, so there is nothing to request. The only record of your use is the progress file on your own device, which is under your control.

Changes to this policy

If an app ever does start collecting something, that app's own page below will say so plainly and the change will be described here before the version that does it ships.
