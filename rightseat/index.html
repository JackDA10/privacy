RightSeat

This app collects no data about you or your students, and makes no network requests of its own. Your records stay on your device.

What this app stores

On your device only, in the app's own storage. None of it is sent anywhere by the app.

  • Your training records. The students you add, with their lessons, sessions, ACS marks, endorsements, notes and photos; your courses, aircraft and contacts; and your instructor details, which are printed on the records you share.
  • Document scans and photos. Certificates, medicals and other papers you scan with the camera or pick from your photo library. They are kept as files beside the records and are never read, uploaded or analyzed.
  • A few device preferences. Whether expiry reminders are on, your attention windows, whether you have seen the first-launch screens, and whether a student's extra sections are open.

To remove records, delete a student (with all their records), a course, a contact or a document where it is listed. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. Your iPhone's own backup to iCloud or to a computer includes the app's storage, as it does for any app.

Permissions

  • Camera, only when you choose to scan a document.
  • Photos. You pick images in the system photo picker, which hands the app only the photos you choose.
  • Contacts. The app never asks for your address book. When you add a contact from it, the system contact picker hands over only the one you choose.
  • Notifications, if you turn on expiry reminders. They are scheduled on the device.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests of its own, and no syncing. It works fully in airplane mode. What can leave the device is listed below, and each item starts with something you tap.

What can leave the device, when you tap it

  • Sharing and backups. A study handout, a training record, a student transfer file, a session log or a backup goes only where you send it from the share sheet.
  • Study links. Study handouts link to the FAA's own handbooks and regulations on faa.gov and ecfr.gov, which open in your browser.
  • Call, text and email. These buttons hand the number or address to Phone, Messages or Mail.
  • Report a bug and Request a feature open a Mail draft with the app and iOS versions and the device model filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it.
  • Privacy policy, Support and Terms of Use open in your browser.

Nothing about you or your students travels with a link. Once a page is open, that site's own privacy practices apply rather than this policy.

Purchases

RightSeat is free for a limited number of active students. RightSeat Unlimited is an optional subscription, sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and the app keeps nothing about it beyond the App Store's own signed transaction. Manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings, or from Manage subscription in the app's Settings.
