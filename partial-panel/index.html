Partial Panel Trainer

This app collects no data about you and makes no network connections of its own.

What this app stores

On your device only: one small record of how you have scored on each kind of failure drill — vacuum failures, electrical failures, pitot-static blockages and the rest — so the drill can bring back the ones you keep missing. Nothing else. There is no name, no identifier and no history of individual questions. It never leaves the device.

This app has no reset control yet. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests — every instrument, diagram, drill and regulation is inside the app, and it works fully in airplane mode
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or health access — the app has no permission prompt at all
  • No purchases or subscriptions

Links to other apps

The More from AutoPilot screen lists the other apps in this family and links to the App Store and to flywithautopilot.com. Tapping one hands that link to your browser or to the App Store app. The app itself sends nothing: no identifier, no progress, nothing about you travels with that link. Once the page is open, that site's privacy practices apply rather than this one's.

Support

Questions, bugs, or an instrument you think the app has wrong: admin@flywithautopilot.com. Say which screen and which instrument, and include your device and iOS version.

Partial Panel Trainer teaches how a light-aircraft six-pack fails — which instruments a vacuum pump feeds, which the electrical bus feeds, and what the remaining needles show once one of them is gone. Use it to learn the pattern, not as the authority for any particular airplane: your aircraft's own systems, its POH and its placards decide what actually happens in it.
