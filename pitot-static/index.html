Pitot-Static

This app collects no data about you and makes no network connections.

What this app stores

One thing, on your device only: which chapters you have opened and your best score in each quiz section. It is kept in the app's own storage, never leaves the phone or iPad, and is removed when you delete the app. The chapter list has a reset control that clears it at any time.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests — the app contains no networking code, so it works identically in airplane mode
  • No purchases or subscriptions

About the content

The lessons are written from the FAA's own handbooks — the Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (FAA‑H‑8083‑25C), the Instrument Procedures Handbook (FAA‑H‑8083‑16B) — and from Title 14 of the Code of Federal Regulations. All diagrams are drawn in the app itself.

This is a study aid for training, not an operational reference. Fly the aircraft's own approved flight manual, its checklists, and current regulations.
