Clip Shear

This app collects no data about you and makes no network connections of its own. Your videos are cleaned on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Your videos

You choose a video with Apple's photo picker, or share one to Clip Shear from another app. Clip Shear works on a temporary copy on your device, and deletes that copy once the cleaned video is ready. Nothing is sent anywhere.

If you turn on Remove sound, the cleaned video is saved without its sound, and the sound can be saved on its own as an audio file. Both are made on your iPhone.

Clip Shear asks for two kinds of Photos access, each only when you use the feature that needs it:

  • Add only, when you tap Save to Photos. It lets the app add the cleaned video to your library. It cannot see your other photos.
  • Full access, only if you tap Delete the original video. It is needed to find and remove the original. iOS asks you to confirm the deletion, and the original stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

What this app stores

On your device only: how many free cleans you have used today, and a short log of the steps taken when you share a video to the app, used to troubleshoot sharing. It never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app. The app has no separate control to clear it; the count resets on its own each day.

Purchases

Clip Shear is free to use for three cleans a day. Clip Shear Premium is a one-time in-app purchase that removes the limit. Apple handles the payment; the app only learns whether the purchase has been made, so it can unlock Premium and restore it on your other iPhones. No payment details ever reach the app.

Links out of the app

View in Photos opens Apple's Photos app on your iPhone. Share, and Save the sound as audio, hand the file to the iOS share sheet, and whatever app you pick there receives it under its own privacy practices. Privacy & Support opens this page in your browser. Nothing else travels with any of them.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests of its own
  • No uploading of your videos
