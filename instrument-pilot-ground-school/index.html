Instrument Pilot Ground School

This app collects no data about you and makes no network requests of its own. Nothing leaves your device.

What this app stores

Progress, in the app's private storage on your device (UserDefaults), readable only by this app. The developer never receives any of it.

  • Today's 10 review cards and which you have answered (stored as groundschool.review.v1).
  • Your review accuracy by topic, used to weight future cards (stored as groundschool.review.stats.v1).
  • When the app last asked you for a rating: the app version that asked and the date it asked. The app asks only right after you pass a quiz, and these two records keep it from asking too often. They hold nothing else.
  • Which IFR Lost Comms lessons you finished (stored as lostcomms.lessons.v1).
  • Your IFR Lost Comms quiz accuracy by topic (stored as lostcomms.weakness).
  • Whether you have seen the Lost Comms introduction (stored as lostcomms.guide.seen.v1).
  • Your VOR and HSI drill accuracy by topic (stored as vortrainer.weakness.v1).
  • Your Partial Panel drill accuracy by topic (stored as partialpanel.weakness.v1).

Deleting the app deletes all of these. If your device is backed up to your own iCloud or computer, this storage may be part of that backup under your own Apple account.

What this app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No network requests of its own
  • No purchases or subscriptions

Links out of the app

These open in Safari or the App Store only when you tap them: this privacy page and its support section, and App Store pages for other apps by this developer (Racetrack and Private Pilot Ground School). Those sites have their own policies.

The app itself sends nothing with any of these: no identifier, no progress, nothing about you.

Rating prompt

After a passing result the app may ask Apple's system to show its standard rating prompt. Apple controls whether it appears and does not tell the app what you answered.

Children

The app is a study aid for pilots and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Not affiliated with the FAA

This app is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Aviation Administration. It is a study aid and is not for navigation or real flight.

Support

Questions about this policy, found an error in the content, or something not working right? Please write to admin@flywithautopilot.com. Say which lesson and what it should say, and include your device and iOS version.
